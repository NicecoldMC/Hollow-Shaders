# Hollow-Shaders
A Minecraft Shader Pack
A shader pack for Iris that gives Minecraft’s sky, light and water a big upgrade. The clouds are the main feature.

Features

Clouds and sky

Volumetric 3D clouds with billowy cumulus shapes, soft edges, and sunlit tops with shaded undersides
Clouds that react to the weather: clear skies, a darker gray overcast in rain and storms, and dark clouds at night
A smooth horizon gradient and golden sunrises and sunsets with a glowing sun

Lighting and shadows

Soft shadows with a sharpness setting
Sunlight that changes colour: warm at sunset, blue at night
Shade that picks up the sky colour, with stronger contrast between light and shadow
Coloured glow from torches and other light sources

Water and lava

Water that reflects the sky and clouds, going from clear to turquoise to deep blue with depth
Shoreline foam and sun glints
A rippling view of the sky when you look up from underwater
Dense, glowing fog inside lava

Everything else

Vivid colours with adjustable saturation, plus bloom
Waving grass and leaves
Normal Nether and End looks, with a dark End void
